System Overview

Documented Systems

Oven Systems

The manual covers bake belts, combustion and pilot systems, air-knife/infeed equipment, insulation, crumb trays, doors, hood assemblies, ventilation, cleaning, maintenance, troubleshooting, and lubrication.

Controls

Documented components include Allen-Bradley PanelView controls, a safety relay, PowerFlex 525 AC drive, Fireye flame-safety components, and a Watlow high-temperature controller.

Pre-Sheeter Feed System

The pre-sheeter uses textured rolls, anti-bridging paddles, an adjustable roll gap, a doctor blade, and independent drives for the rolls and paddles.

Maintenance Focus

Key maintenance areas include belt tension and alignment, lubrication, combustion adjustment, drive components, bearings, seals, paddles, controls, and sanitation.
