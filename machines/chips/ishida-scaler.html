Weighing Process

Combination Weighing Overview

1. Dispersion TableReceives incoming product and distributes it toward the radial feeder pans.
2. Radial FeedersVibrate to move product from the dispersion area into the pool hoppers.
3. Pool HoppersTemporarily hold product before releasing it into the weigh hoppers.
4. Weigh HoppersLoad cells measure the product in each hopper.
5. Combination CalculationThe controller evaluates available hopper weights and selects the combination closest to the target package weight.
6. DischargeThe selected product is released through the discharge chute to the bagger or downstream packaging machine.
