System Overview

Main Assemblies

Drive Assembly

Includes the motor, reducer, drive box, reducer mounting plate, auger connector, connector roll pin, discharge transition, gaskets, and protective covers.

Auger Assembly

The manual documents the casing, compression couplings, Helix auger, and optional center core. Auger styles include round wire, flat wire, beveled edge, and heavy-duty square bar.

Hopper Assembly

Documented hopper configurations include removable or hinged bar grates, safety interlocks, low-level sensing, hopper vibrator, boot weldment, and boot end cap.

Controls & Maintenance

The manual covers installation, electrical panel operation, startup, preventive maintenance, troubleshooting, mechanical/electrical spares, sensors, remote-start options, and interlocks.
