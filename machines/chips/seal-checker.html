System Overview

Main Components

Product Infeed

J-Belt Conveyor: receives packaged product from the upstream packer and transfers it to the infeed conveyor.

Infeed Conveyor: carries product into the seal-check section.

Product Conditioning

Brush Conditioner: stabilizes and flattens the package before it enters the seal-check device.

Cleaning Brush: removes dirt and debris from the conveyor belt when fitted.

Seal Inspection

Lower Seal Check Conveyor: carries the package through the measurement area.

Upper Seal Check Conveyor: applies load to the passing package so thickness changes can be measured.

Seal Check Device: evaluates package thickness for seal quality.

Reject System

Photoelectric Sensors: detect product presence and movement through the inspection section.

Reject Conveyor / Air Jet: directs accepted or rejected product according to the machine judgment.
